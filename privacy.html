Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Overview

AGM Media is a creator-content workflow used to prepare, review, and publish short-form media through authorized third-party platform integrations.

2. Information we process

Depending on the features used, AGM Media may process:

  • account identifiers and profile information returned by an authorized platform;
  • access and refresh tokens required to maintain an authorized connection;
  • videos, captions, publishing settings, and related content metadata;
  • technical logs needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the requested content-management and publishing functionality, maintain authorized integrations, troubleshoot errors, and protect the service.

4. Third-party platforms

AGM Media may connect to third-party platforms, including TikTok, only after the user authorizes the connection. Use of those platforms remains subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Data sharing

AGM Media does not sell personal information. Data is shared only when necessary to provide an authorized integration, comply with law, protect the service, or when the user directs us to do so.

6. Data retention and security

Data is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the service to operate or to meet legal and security obligations. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect credentials and account data.

7. Disconnecting an account and deletion

Users may revoke a third-party authorization through the relevant platform. Requests relating to data associated with AGM Media may be directed to the operator through github.com/melvillelee.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as AGM Media changes. The date above will be revised when a material update is published.
